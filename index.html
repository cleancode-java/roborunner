Welcome to Robo Runner!

Robo Runner is an exciting and fast-paced game where you control a robot and try to survive as long as possible. The main goal is to run, avoid obstacles, and get the highest score.

In this game, you need to react quickly and make fast decisions. The robot moves forward, and you must jump or avoid different obstacles. The game becomes more difficult over time, which makes it more interesting and challenging.

Game Features:
- Simple and fun gameplay
- Increasing difficulty
- Score system
- Easy control
- Play directly in your browser
